# Marketing plan

Marketing for this product is mostly relationships and proof. Paid ads come last.

## 1. Positioning

**For:** colleges whose students have weak, expensive or shared internet.
**Promise:** classes that keep working when the connection does not.
**Proof:** audio fallback in live classes, downloads and offline assignments, notes in the student's own language.
**Not:** another video-call tool, and not "AI for education". Lead with the problem, mention AI as one feature.

Taglines to test: "Classes that don't stop when the internet does." / "Every student in the class, whatever the signal."

## 2. Audiences and channels

| Audience | Channel | First action |
|---|---|---|
| Principals, directors | Direct introductions, college-management WhatsApp groups, association meetings, a short LinkedIn presence | Ask each pilot principal for two introductions |
| HoDs and teachers | Teacher WhatsApp/Telegram groups, YouTube tutorials in Telugu/Hindi, college workshops | A free 45-minute workshop: "Teaching with weak internet" |
| Students | Campus ambassadors, college fests, Instagram reels in local languages | A named ambassador per pilot college with a certificate |
| Institutions that fund colleges | State higher-education councils, skilling missions, incubators, CSR programmes aimed at rural connectivity | Short deck + pilot results; apply to the next call |

## 3. Content (start in month 2 while pilots run)

| Week | Piece | Format |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Why classes break in villages (and what we did about it)" | 600-word post + 2-minute video |
| 2 | Teacher quick-start in Telugu and Hindi | 3-minute screen recording |
| 3 | Real numbers from week 3 of the pilot (anonymised) | One image + caption |
| 4 | "How to run an online class on a 2G student's phone" | Checklist PDF (shareable, useful without LanternED) |
| 6 | Champion teacher story | Short interview video (with consent) |
| 8 | Principal's view: before/after | Quote card + one-pager |
| Then | Weekly: one tip, one teacher story, one number | WhatsApp status / LinkedIn |

Reuse everything: one pilot interview becomes a post, a quote card, a proposal paragraph and a conference-deck slide.

## 4. Website (build by early February)

Single page, mobile-first and light (your audience is on slow phones):
1. Headline and a 20-second silent demo loop of the slow-network audio fallback.
2. Three benefits with icons: stays on, works offline, speaks your language.
3. How it works for a college: upload class list, teachers teach, you see reports.
4. Pricing summary with "Start a free pilot".
5. Privacy and data location, grievance officer contact.
6. Pilot college logos and quotes (only with written permission).
7. Contact: WhatsApp button first, form second.

## 5. Launch-week checklist (22-28 February)

- [ ] Production checklist in `docs/OPERATIONS.md` section 1 fully ticked; restore drill done this month
- [ ] Support rota for launch week (two people, college hours, WhatsApp number published)
- [ ] Status page or pinned message for incidents
- [ ] Privacy notice, terms and consent text reviewed by counsel and linked in the app footer
- [ ] Website live; pricing page live; demo college reset and rehearsed
- [ ] Two case studies written and approved by the colleges
- [ ] Play Store listing approved (see `PLAY_STORE.md`)
- [ ] Announcement post, three short videos, and personal messages to the 30 warmest contacts
- [ ] Ten demos booked before announcing publicly
- [ ] Usage dashboard open on launch day; someone watching errors and live minutes

## 6. Metrics

| Stage | Metric | Starting target |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Workshop attendees, video views | 150 teachers reached in the first two months |
| Interest | Demo requests | 20 per month by launch |
| Trial | Pilots started | 3 by mid-December, 10 by end of March |
| Adoption | Teachers active weekly in each pilot | 60% |
| Revenue | Paying colleges, ARR, cost per active student | 3 signed by end of January; track margin per college |
| Satisfaction | Teacher "would you recommend" score | 8 of 10 or above |

## 7. Partnerships worth one conversation each

Education-focused incubators; teacher-training institutes; NGOs working on rural connectivity; telecom or device partners that can bundle data for students; state university affiliation offices (who can recommend tools to affiliated colleges).

## 8. Things not to do yet

Paid social ads, a large sales team, a freemium consumer app, and feature promises you have not built. Say "we are building X; tell us if you need it" and write the answers down: they are your roadmap.
